# Astra editorial explainer frame

Use a warm paper field for the argument and a near-black field for the hard
floor / approval beat. Keep the UI imagery real and rectangular. Type should
carry the story; annotations should point at evidence instead of decorating it.

Avoid neon gradients, centered floating card stacks, fake chat transcripts, and
generic AI imagery. Every scene needs a clear editorial label, one dominant
claim, and one piece of evidence.
