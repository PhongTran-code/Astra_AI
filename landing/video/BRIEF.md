## Intent

Rebuild the landing-page showcase as a script-led, Vox-inspired editorial
explainer rather than a continuous AI-looking screen recording. The story must
be legible in one pass: a user gives Astra a real task, Astra plans and works,
the human approves a consequential action, and Astra hands back a finished
result with an audit trail. Keep the visual language sharp, annotated, slightly
tactile, and grounded in Astra's real UI.
